# Fuentes — 2026-11-01_mxenes-ia-descubrimiento

## SRC-001
- **Cita**: Shamshirgar, A. S. et al. (2026). Data-driven discovery and rapid, direct synthesis of MXenes. *Science* 394, eaeh5957.
- **DOI**: 10.1126/science.aeh5957
- **URL**: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aeh5957
- **Respalda**: Machine learning–assisted database mining identified 33 previously synthesized but unrecognized MXene candidates. Guided by findings, synthesized seven multilayer MXenes using rapid self-propagating high-temperature method (completes in minutes, no sustained external heating). Further realized 11 previously unexplored rare earth–based MXenes (Pr, Nd, Sm, Gd, Tb, Ho, Tm). Experiments and modeling suggest semiconducting behavior and diverse magnetic states.

## SRC-002
- **Cita**: Linköping University (2026, 29 Oct). Hidden and forgotten 2D materials are discovered with AI.
- **URL**: https://liu.se/en/news-item/gomda-och-glomda-2d-material-upptacks-med-ai
- **Respalda**: Researchers Johanna Rosén and Ali Saffar Shamshirgar at Linköping University used AI and supercomputers. Identified 33 existing MXenes and created 11 new ones. Project unofficially called “the Treasure Chest Project”. MXenes: few atomic layers, extremely large surface area relative to weight. New synthesis method easier, fewer hazardous chemicals.
